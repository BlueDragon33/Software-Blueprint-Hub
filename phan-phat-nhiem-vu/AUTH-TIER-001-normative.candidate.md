# AUTH-TIER-001 — Normative candidate (NOT ACTIVE)

**Scope:** Universal Constitution 1.3.0 baseline, amendment ID AUTH-TIER-001. **Status: DRAFT / UNRATIFIED / NOT-PUBLISHED.** Text is a proposed additive rule set, not a change to the published Constitution or application runtime. New R-number and target policy version are **UNASSIGNED**, to be reconciled with open PRs #147, #151–#156 (R33/R34 and associated authority work) and any other amendments before final human review.

## Proposed universal requirements — AUTH-01…AUTH-12

**AUTH-01 — Explicit classification.** Every governed Project Profile shall declare whether its app account model is `AUTH-L1`, `AUTH-L2`, `AUTH-PLANE` or `NOT-APPLICABLE` once this amendment is published and its compatible migration is applied. Classification is not a runtime login mandate. Distinguish it from Blueprint Level B0–B5, app topology and local roles. The Profile must record product owner, evidence source, managed mode (`required`/`optional`/`not-applicable`) and migration decision. New repos enter through existing onboarding. An unknown/unverified app remains pending, not silently L1-compliant.

**AUTH-02 — AUTH-L1.** One app-user account tier, time-bounded entitlement for managed features. The application owns the account and its data. Stable `accountId`, explicit lifecycle, `startsAt`, `expiresAt`, cancellation/revocation and owner-configurable renewal are mandatory **when managed accounts are enabled**. No implicit delegated account manager is created merely by having editor, guardian or viewer roles. Offline-only local core may remain account-free under a reviewed optional model.

**AUTH-03 — AUTH-L2.** Adds an explicitly delegated, scope-bounded manager who approves/manages lower-tier users. Delegated account authority is distinct from App Owner, Constitutional Authority, Project Authority, Production Authority and system/device administration. Manager cannot grant their own authority, expand scope, approve self/peer/superior, inspect credentials or make cross-app decisions. Real authorization is enforced at trusted server boundaries. Two distinct onboarding decisions (request and profile activation) are the default reference for managed L2; alternative flows require independently reviewed equivalence, reason and complete audit.

**AUTH-04 — Authority and data ownership.** Application Management may configure/apply app-scoped typed owner policy, delegated scopes, quotas and readback metadata. It shall not become a canonical store for app profile, health, children, media, passport, financial, study, credentials or secret material. No global identity service or SSO is implied by adoption; cross-app federation requires a separate security decision.

**AUTH-05 — Credentials and sessions.** Authenticated app-user credentials are stored as per-secret salted and benchmarked slow password hashes or an independently reviewed equivalent, never decryptable/recoverable plaintext. Privileged actors cannot view another person's password; recovery uses short-lived single-use tokens/invitations. Security-sensitive operations require server-side role/scope checks, anti-CSRF/origin controls, rate limiting, rotated/revocable sessions, audit and proportionate MFA/step-up for Owner/delegated manager.

**AUTH-06 — Expiry and quotas.** Managed access checks expiration and suspension/revocation on the authoritative boundary, not by the UI clock or local storage. UTC timestamps and append-only changes; expiration never silently auto-renews. Per-app configurable default duration, maximum accounts by role, devices where supported and other resource limits use versioned/atomic concurrency guards. Rollback of one policy change may not resurrect revoked rights. No global fixed 30-day or 3-device mandate.

**AUTH-07 — Device and consent-based synchronization.** Device identity/keys are app-scoped and distinct from account identity; IP/browser fingerprint are not authorization. Where multi-device sync exists: explicit new-device enrollment, scoped consent for data categories, server-owned authoritative state, idempotency keys, ACK and revision/CAS conflict handling. Revocation blocks new trusted requests; a disconnected copy cannot be guaranteed erased. Never share private device keys or blindly copy IndexedDB/localStorage/PII.

**AUTH-08 — Local-first and privacy.** Preserve the Project Profile's verified offline-local capabilities when adding optional managed-auth. Unreachable services must fail closed for **protected remote operations**, while allowed offline core continues under explicit data risk/reconciliation rules. Account expiry cannot be bypassed for protected server actions; sensitive cached data needs retention/deletion/lock policy appropriate to the app. Child/health data require additional scoped review.

**AUTH-09 — Owner UI and FREE boundary.** Per-app owner policies (duration, renewal, quota, delegate scope and rollout flags) must be configurable from authorized Application Management UI **when the integration is available and approved**, with authenticated readback and audit; no routine code edits to change durations. The app remains on existing/free infrastructure within verified limits; zero paid upgrade/extra hosted dependencies without separate authority.

**AUTH-10 — Feature rollout and migration.** Default flag OFF; additive/idempotent migrations preserve account, legacy data, certificate/history and existing URLs. No heuristic identity merging. Provide migration count reconciliation, backup/restore, canary, rollback, compatibility/deprecation window and app-specific owner approval before enabling protected features. A Constitution adoption event never itself activates login or grants Production authority.

**AUTH-11 — Acceptance gates.** Proof must include API negative authorization (self/peer/scope), onboarding/expiry/revoke/recovery/session, atomic quota races, real device and consented sync where applicable, privacy isolation, representative browser/mobile UX, offline/degraded functionality, migration rehearsal+restore, rate limits, target-runtime cost capacity and independent review. `NOT_RUN`/`BLOCKED` must not be converted to `PASS`.

**AUTH-12 — Governance separation.** The canonical Constitutional Authority protocol controls amendment ratification, coordinated publication of normative text/contract/template and staged propagation. App Managers cannot ratify Constitution; AI cannot approve itself. Authentication/delegation tier never changes Project/Production/Constitutional grants. Every app has a project-local Work Package after publication; governed compliance is not evidence of Production deployment.

## Compatibility and exclusions

Additive classification with opt-in per-app enforcement is a **candidate compatible strengthening**, not yet a final version-class decision. A compulsory cloud-login, shared identity database or removal of core offline access would be a breaking change and is **out of scope**. Libraries without end-user accounts may be NOT-APPLICABLE; control-plane apps are AUTH-PLANE. Review conflicts with #155/#156 prompt-contract proposals and #154 signed review authority: use existing authority primitives, no new issuer. Resolve R-number and target version only after exact source-set review.
