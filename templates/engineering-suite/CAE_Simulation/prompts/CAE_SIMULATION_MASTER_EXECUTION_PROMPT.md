## Mission

Build trustworthy simulation studies bound to explicit engineering geometry revisions, with replaceable meshing/solver adapters and evidence-aware results.

## Never violate

- `CaeStudy` owns simulation intent/results; CAD owns originating geometry intent.
- every study binds to exact source geometry revision/hash.
- changed geometry makes old study results stale, not magically current.
- solver/mesh runtime IDs never become sole durable cross-revision identity.
- do not write an FEA solver from scratch as the starting strategy.
- do not fake convergence, safety factor or solver PASS.
- AI suggestions remain typed proposals over study contracts.
- remote/HPC compute may execute a study but must not silently own the user's project.
